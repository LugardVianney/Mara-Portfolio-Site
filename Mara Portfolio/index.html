The Last Message

An original short story built around a breakup message and the emotional weight of shared memories. Written to demonstrate emotional hooks and reader engagement.

  • Developed the premise and emotional conflict.
  • Wrote an attention-grabbing opening.
  • Used concise dialogue and relatable details.
  • Built tension around the final message.
  • Structured the story for possible social media serialisation.

Excerpt

PART 1: FOUR WORDS THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

At exactly 11:47 p.m., her phone vibrated.

She picked it up, expecting his usual goodnight message.

But what she saw made her heart stop.

“I think we should end this.”

Read the rest

She read it once.

Twice.

Then a third time, hoping the words would somehow change.

Three years.

Three years of memories, promises, late-night calls, laughter, arguments, and dreams about forever.

And all he needed to end it were four words.

She typed, “Why?”

Deleted it.

Typed, “Did I do something wrong?”

Deleted that too.

Finally, she gathered the courage to ask the question that had been eating her up for months.

“Did you ever really love me?”

For several minutes, there was silence.

Then the typing bubbles appeared.

Disappeared.

Appeared again.

Her heart raced.

Finally, his reply came.

“I did. I just stopped knowing how.”

She dropped her phone onto the bed.

Tears rolled down her cheeks.

But something about his response bothered her.

If he had stopped knowing how to love her, when did it happen?

Was it when he started ignoring her calls?

When his replies became shorter?

Or when she began feeling lonely even while she was still in a relationship?

She wiped her tears and opened their old pictures.

Then she noticed something she had never paid attention to before.

In almost every recent picture, she was smiling.

But he wasn’t looking at her.

He was always looking somewhere else.

She stared at one picture for a long time.

Then her phone vibrated again.

It was him.

One final message.

“There’s something I should have told you a long time ago.”

Her hands began to shake.

She opened the message.

And what she read next changed everything.

TO BE CONTINUED…

Sometimes, the hardest part of losing someone isn’t saying goodbye. It’s discovering what they kept from you while you were still holding on.

A Note to the Woman I’m Becoming

A reflective story about self-worth, rebuilding confidence, and choosing progress during difficult seasons, written to create an intimate connection with readers.

  • Chose a relatable personal-growth theme.
  • Created a warm, honest narrative voice.
  • Connected struggle with hope and practical reflection.
  • Wrote a memorable closing message suitable for social media.

Excerpt

Dear future me, I hope you are proud of how far we’ve come.

Dear woman I’m becoming,

I hope you remember the girl who kept going even when life gave her every reason to give up.

I hope you remember the nights she cried silently because she didn’t know how things would work out.

Read the rest

The mornings she woke up with a heavy heart but still found the strength to face another day.

The dreams she kept private because she was afraid people would laugh at how big they were.

I hope you never forget her.

She wasn’t perfect. She made mistakes, trusted the wrong people, questioned her worth and sometimes settled for less than she deserved.

But she was trying.

Trying to understand herself. Trying to build a better life. Trying to become someone she could look at in the mirror and genuinely admire.

I know the journey won’t always be easy.

There will be moments when you’ll feel behind, when your achievements seem small and when you’ll wonder if all your efforts are worth it.

But please, don’t be too hard on yourself.

You are allowed to grow slowly.

You are allowed to change your mind.

You are allowed to outgrow people, places and versions of yourself that no longer reflect who you are.

And most importantly, you are allowed to start again.

I hope you become a woman who chooses peace over approval, purpose over pressure and self-respect over temporary happiness.

I hope you build the life you once prayed for.

But even if everything doesn’t happen exactly as planned, I hope you never stop believing in yourself.

Until then, keep growing. Keep learning. Keep showing up.

I’m not where I want to be yet, but I’m no longer where I used to be.

And that, in itself, is progress.

With love,
The woman who refused to give up.

The Small Business Behind the Scent

A proposed brand story showing how a small perfume business can communicate its personality, customer experience, and value through warm, customer-focused storytelling. This is a concept sample.

  • Framed the brand story around identity and confidence.
  • Used customer-focused language.
  • Connected fragrance with mood and self-expression.
  • Developed copy suitable for a social media post or an About section.

Excerpt

Every beautiful scent has a story. This one began with a dream.

It started with a simple desire: to make people feel beautiful, confident and unforgettable.

What looked like a small perfume business to the outside world was something much bigger to the woman behind it.

It was a dream built from little savings, late nights, countless prayers and the courage to start something of her own.

Read the rest

There were days when orders came in, and there were days when her phone remained silent.

There were moments she questioned whether she was doing enough, whether people would trust her brand and whether her little business would ever become something remarkable.

But she kept going.

Every perfume bottle she packaged carried more than fragrance. It carried dedication, hope and a piece of a dream she refused to abandon.

She wasn’t just selling perfumes. She was helping people create memories, express their personalities and leave a beautiful impression wherever they went.

With every customer, every order and every recommendation, the dream grew a little stronger.

Today, the journey is still unfolding.

The brand may be small, but the vision has never been small.

Because behind every bottle is a woman who dared to begin, even when she didn’t have everything figured out.

This is more than a fragrance business. It’s a story of passion, courage and the beauty of starting small.

Small beginnings. Beautiful impressions. A scent worth remembering.
